# Resume HTML is the source of truth for facts

The Site copies its Claims from the Resume, never the other way round, and where the Resume and LinkedIn disagree the Resume wins (Faire title, Shopify location and Ottawa were reconciled on 2026-11-09). A build-time check warns when Site Role data drifts from the Resume. The Resume's company-neutral vocabulary and Claim rules apply to the Site; the only extra named product is Shopify Flow, which is public.
